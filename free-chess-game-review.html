Questions

Is BlunderCheck really free?

Yes. Stockfish analysis of your games is unlimited and free, with the accuracy report, the decisive moments, guided lessons and opening statistics. The AI coach comments 3 of your games every month for free; BlunderCheck+ (€1.50 a month billed yearly, or €2.99 monthly) goes up to 100 commented games a month.

Do my games leave my browser?

No. Your games stay in your browser and Stockfish runs there. A game is only sent to the AI coach when you ask for a comment or ask the coach a question.

Does it work with Lichess and Chess.com?

Yes. Enter your Chess.com or Lichess username to load your latest games, or paste a PGN.
